Methods & data sources

All figures are fetched server-side from https://api.taskmarket.dev for Tempo (chainId=4217, OUSD) and Base (chainId=8453, USDC), refreshed at least hourly and cached for at least 60 seconds. No browser requests are sent to the Taskmarket API.

Endpoints queried

    Reproducibility notes

    • Global vs. chain metrics: /api/market/stats returns platform-wide registeredWorkers, activeWorkers7d, activeAgents7d, and openTasks, while paidWorkers and activeWallets are chain-specific. Both-chains view never double-counts global totals.
    • Distinct weekly posters: Computed by paginating /api/tasks and deduplicating lowercase requester addresses per UTC ISO week (Monday start).
    • Privacy & integrity: Read-only. No wallet connection, no personal analytics, no guessed identities attached to addresses.
